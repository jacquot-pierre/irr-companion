**Which side mission holds the story**: the MISSIONS tab now shows that *Reconnaissance - Traders* must be done
for the story to go on.

**MISSIONS** (app and web)
- The game counts *Reconnaissance - Traders* as a story mission (its `bStoryMission` flag, read from the game
  files): the story stops at the next yellow bar until it is done. It is now yellow in the tree (**! Recon
  Traders**), named under every yellow bar, and its details say it is needed. If *Opening Eyes - Part 2* does not
  come after *Dirty Work*, finish it (trade with every trader).
- The other reconnaissance missions (Delta, Quarry, Outskirts, Bunker) say they are not needed for the story.
- An objective with "&" (*Find & Retrieve the second Antenna Piece*) is no longer cut.

**Item prices**: a new ITEMS tab lists every item of the game with its buy and sell prices.

**ITEMS** (new tab, app and web)
- The 840 items of the game by category (weapons, weapon parts, ammunition, grenades, melee, gear, medical, food and
  drinks, barter, containers, gadgets, keys, quest items, currency) and sub-category, with their icon.
- **Market**: the price to buy it, and the faction loyalty level the market asks for.
- **Black market**: its price in Marked Coins.
- **Trader**: the price at a raid trader and which traders can have it in stock (weapons are not listed).
- **Sell**: what the market pays you: the price x the share of its category (weapons 35 %, parts 45 %, gear and ammo
  40 %, barter 80 %, keys 20 %, others 60 %). Grey with "?": the game files say it cannot be sold at the market
  (most barter items, keys), to check in game.
- **Trader pays**: what a raid trader gives you for the quest crates (Chemical Crate 800, Data Storage Crate 760,
  Weapon Crate 690).
- Weapons are priced new, with their default parts; ammunition per round. Click a column to sort, hover a row for
  the details, search by name.

Also: about 120 more item icons (barter, food, medical, crates...), also in the mission rewards.

If you have v1.5.0 or later, the **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button next to the tabs installs this version in one click.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds, hideout progress and settings are kept. Game data: version 1.4.0.3
(2026-11-12). Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

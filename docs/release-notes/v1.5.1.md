The app now opens on the **MAPS** tab.

- **MAPS first**: the app (and the web version) starts on the maps instead of the presets editor. The other tabs
  are where they were.
- **Maps**: the whole map is shown when the window opens or changes size, until you zoom or move it (it could open
  zoomed in on a corner).

If you have v1.5.0, the **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button next to the tabs installs this version in one click.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds, hideout progress and settings are kept. Game data: version 1.4.0.1
(2026-11-06). Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

**Player spawns** (MAPS tab, app and web)
- Every spawn you can pick when deploying, in blue with its name: *Train Track, Highway, Lakeside, Logs* (Delta),
  *Fishing Village, Riverside, Warehouse* (Quarry), *Rice Fields, Docks, Huts, Rail Bridge, Coast* (Outskirts),
  *Medbay, Tunnelsystem, Exterior* (Bunker, each on its floor). Your squad starts around that point.

  "SpawnZoneD"): they are named from their place (Riverside is on the river bank) and marked with a "?".

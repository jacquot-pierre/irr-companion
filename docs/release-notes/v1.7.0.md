**Weapon stats**: the weapon builder shows the stats of your build, and every part changes them.

**STATS** (WEAPONS tab, app and web)
- Ergonomics, recoil, weight, accuracy, muzzle velocity, fire rate, effective range and sighting range of the
  build, read from the game files (every weapon, part and ammo has its own stats).
- Every part changes them as in the game: weight (kg) and ergonomics (points) are added; recoil, muzzle velocity
  and some ergonomics change by a percentage; a sight gives its own sighting range (e.g. 800 m for an ACOG).
- Bars for ergonomics and recoil, with a mark at the weapon's default value; green / red: better / worse than the
  weapon as additem gives it (its default parts).
- The ammo of the build: damage, penetration and piercing level.
- The totals are worked out like the game's stat modifiers; the game may round them differently.

If you have v1.5.0 or later, the **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button next to the tabs installs this version in one click.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds, hideout progress and settings are kept. Game data: version 1.4.0.1
(2026-11-06). Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

A new **TRADERS** tab: what the traders met in raids can have in stock, with the chance and the price of every item.

**Traders** (new tab)
- The traders of the game files with their map: Lt Moroz (IGC, Delta - our guess), Cpt Hale (UICS, Quarry),
  Voss (UICS, Outskirts, same stock as Cpt Hale), Ba Tran (VLF, Bunker).
- **Random loot**, the part that differs between traders: medical items and grenades (IGC), weapon attachments
  (UICS), backpacks, helmets, rigs and plate carriers (VLF), with the rarity tier of each item. The chances are
  our estimate from the game's weights.
- **Random pick**: 6 or 7 of 17 barter items (globe, compass, golden ring, military laptop...), exact chances.
- **Always in stock**: the 35 barter items (60 units) every trader has.
- **Price** of one unit: the market price of the game's vendor table.
- **Find an item**: type part of a name (search after a short pause or Enter, Esc clears) to see which trader can
  have it. Weapons are not listed, only the chance of having one.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds and hideout progress are kept. Game data: build of 2026-11-06.
Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

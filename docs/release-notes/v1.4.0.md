**Find an item** (MAPS tab)
- Type an item (graphics card, bandage, golden ring...) and the map shows a heatmap of where it can spawn: every
  loot container of the map (crates, jackets, medical boxes, ammo boxes, weapon cases, safes, dead bodies...) and
  every loose loot point, weighted by its chance to have the item.
- The line above the map gives the expected number per raid on the map and the chance in each kind of container
  (e.g. graphics card on Delta: 0.31 % per rare crate, 0.04 % per crate).
- Works on every map and on each floor of the Bunker. Enter picks the first match, Esc or × hides the heatmap.
- The chances are our estimate from the game's loot settings (number of items, weight of each kind of item,
  rarity tiers). Loot bonuses of some areas or raids (night, more collectibles, more rarity) are not included.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds and hideout progress are kept. Game data: build of 2026-11-06.
Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

**Locked doors** (MAPS tab, app and web)
- Most locked doors of Delta and Quarry take the key of their door type (*Delta General Key*, *Quarry General
  Key*) instead of a key set on the door itself; they were missing. Now on the maps: **8 on Delta** (there were
  none) and **27 on Quarry** (there was one). Hover one to see the key it needs.
- Outskirts (9) and Bunker (4) are unchanged.

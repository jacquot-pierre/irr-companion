Two new tabs, **HIDEOUT** and **MAPS**, and the advanced AI settings.

**Hideout upgrades** (new tab)
- Every hideout unit and level with what it costs: items with their game icon, cash, Concrete, Food, Metal, Oil.
- Requirements: level of another unit, faction loyalty (IGC, UICS, VLF, FANG), player level.
- Your progress: set the level of each unit, your player level and loyalty; saved in the app.
- Shopping list: everything still needed, for the next upgrades only or for every level left.
- GIVE buttons copy the matching console command, if you want to cheat.
- The free level 1 of the Structure and the Shooting Range (their starting state) is not shown: their levels
  count from the first upgrade you pay for.

**Resource maps** (new tab)
- Minimaps of Delta, Quarry, Outskirts and Bunker (one plan per floor), placed like the in-game PDA map.
- Resource spawn points (Concrete, Food, Metal, Oil) with their chance (40 %) and amount, extraction points,
  compact and large safes, HAS doors and locked doors.
- Hover a safe or a door to see the key it needs, with the key's icon. Filters, zoom and pan.

**AI preset editor**
- **ADVANCED** section (closed by default): the 38 settings of the game's AI file that the in-game menu does
  not show (finer perception, light and laser, trigger delay, deliberate misses, suppression, grenades).
  Names and descriptions not given by the game are ours and say so in their tooltip.
- COMPARE: *Advanced* checkbox, with the number of advanced settings that differ.

**Fixes**
- `irr.World.SetWeather`: weather numbers start at 0 (checked in game). v1.0.0 gave the next weather.
- Lists no longer keep the previous item highlighted (hideout units, maps, floors, command categories).

Your presets, favorites and hideout progress from v1.0.0 are kept. Game data: build of 2026-11-06.
Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

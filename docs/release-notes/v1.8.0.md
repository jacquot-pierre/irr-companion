**Story missions**: a new MISSIONS tab shows the FANG story as a tree, with what to do in every mission.

**MISSIONS** (new tab, app and web)
- The 26 story missions from *Mole Hunt* to *Root Canal*, in the order the game gives them, read from the game's
  mission graph. Where the story splits, the two branches are side by side; a yellow bar marks where the next
  mission waits until every story mission given so far is done.
- The reconnaissance side missions (explore Delta, Quarry, Outskirts, Bunker, meet the traders) next to the mission
  that gives them.
- Click a mission: its map and place, the missions before it, the steps in order with their objectives, quest
  items and tips (*Buy the bombs at the marketplace*), the rewards with their icons, and the briefing (closed by
  default: it tells the story).
- The number of enemies to kill is not in the mission text: the game shows it.

If you have v1.5.0 or later, the **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button next to the tabs installs this version in one click.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds, hideout progress and settings are kept. Game data: version 1.4.0.1
(2026-11-06). Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).

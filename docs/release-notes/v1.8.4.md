**Game update 1.4.0.3**: the data of the app was read again from the game files of version 1.4.0.3
(Steam build 26610539, 2026-11-12).

What changed in the game data:
- **MAPS**: Outskirts has **4 extraction points** instead of 5. One Outskirts resource point moved slightly.
- **MAPS heatmap**: more loot places on **Bunker** (558 instead of 524: gear boxes, ammo boxes, weapon accessory
  boxes, rare crates and loose loot) and on **Quarry** (39 more crates).
- **TRADERS**: the **Generator** comes 3 times more often in the random pick of every trader (weight 2 -> 6): about
  68 % instead of 34 % for Lt Moroz; the other items of that list a bit less often.

Unchanged: AI presets and settings, share codes, console commands, weathers, items, hideout upgrades, weapons and
parts, story missions.

If you have v1.5.0 or later, the **UPDATE AVAILABLE** button next to the tabs installs this version in one click.

Your presets, favorites, weapon builds, hideout progress and settings are kept. Game data: version 1.4.0.3
(2026-11-12). Game icons and minimaps are the developers' artwork, shown for reference (see the README).
